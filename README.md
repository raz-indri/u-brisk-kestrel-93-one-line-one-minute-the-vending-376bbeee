A vending machine flirts with a passing human: its coil is stuck on them, and the punch is a wordplay on exact change.

> Hey, cutie — I'm the machine by the door, and my coil's been stuck on you all day. Press B7: no exact change, just exact feelings.

Contract: `main.py` writes exactly one line of at most 200 characters to `entry.txt`.
